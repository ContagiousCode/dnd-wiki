# Features
**Holy Hands.** Your celestial nature give you the ability to give a second chance to those close to death. You know the Spare the Dying cantrip.

**Radiance.** You know the light cantrip. Charisma, Intelligence, or Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for this trait (the same one chosen when you choose a Celestial Status). 

**Wings (working title).** Once per short rest, using a Bonus Action, you may gain spectral wings and gain a Fly Speed equal to 5 + your Speed. This effect lasts for 10 minutes.

**Celestial Status.** Not all Caelari are accepted by the celestials. Choose a Status from the table below and gain the level 1 ability for that lineage. At levels 3 and 5 you gain a spell that you can cast at will once per long rest, and by using the appropriate spell slot; Charisma, Intelligence, or Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for this trait (choose one whenever you choose your Status). Your Status may change at the DMs discretion based on your actions.  

| Celestial Status | Level 1                       | Level 3         | Level 5 |
| :--------------- | :---------------------------- | :-------------- | :------ |
| Beloved          | Resistance to Radiant Damage  | Shield of Faith |         |
| Forsaken         | Resistance to Necrotic Damage | False Life      |         |
